Straight Answers

Frequently asked questions

Plain answers about the financing, the build and how to get started.

What is Let's Talk Renovation?

One team for your whole Renovation. Mars Construction, Mars Construction CSLB #1138550, plans and builds the project. E Mortgage Capital Inc., NMLS #1416824, finances it with the After Renovation Value HELOC. You start with one call, and the builder and your loan advisor work from the same plan and estimate.

How does the After Renovation Value HELOC work?

It's a home equity line of credit based on what your home will be worth once the project is done, not on the equity you have today. The project gets reviewed, your home is appraised as if the work were finished, and the line is sized on that value. One final inspection confirms the work is done.

Do I have to refinance my first mortgage?

No. The line sits behind your first mortgage as a second loan, so your first mortgage, its rate and its payment stay exactly as they are.

How is this different from a regular HELOC or an FHA 203(k) loan?

A regular HELOC is limited by your equity today, which is often too little for a big project. FHA 203(k) and other Renovation mortgages do look at the value after the work, but they replace your first mortgage. This line does both: it's based on the finished value, and it leaves your first mortgage alone.

What kinds of projects qualify?

Kitchens, bathrooms, room additions, interior and exterior Remodels, roofing, siding, decks, porches, pools and outdoor improvements. ADUs qualify too, through Let's Talk ADU. Full teardowns don't qualify.

Which homes qualify, and can I use it right after I buy?

Primary homes and second homes with one or two units qualify. There's no waiting period after a purchase, so you can buy the home and set up the line soon after closing. Rentals and investment-only properties don't qualify; your advisor can walk you through other options for those.

Do I have to use Mars Construction?

No. Having the builder and your loan advisor on one team keeps the plan, the estimate and the financing in step. If you already have a licensed contractor you trust, they go through the same project review: license and insurance, a short questionnaire, the cost estimate and the plans.

Is Mars Construction licensed?

Yes. Mars Construction Industries Corp, Mars Construction CSLB #1138550, holds California Class A (General Engineering), Class B (General Building), C-36 (Plumbing) and C-20 (Heating and Air Conditioning) licenses. You can look it up on the Contractors State License Board website.

How do the rate, costs and payments work?

They depend on your credit, your home and the size of the line, so they're covered one on one. It's a line of credit, so you draw from it as the work moves forward. Before you decide, your advisor gives you the rate, the fees and how repayment works, in writing.

What areas do you serve?

Homes across Southern California: Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, San Diego and Ventura counties.

Are the before and after photos real homes?

No. They are AI-generated illustrations of typical projects, not photos of client homes or finished work. Your design, scope, price and schedule are set in your own written contract.
