How you pay for it

Pay for It With the Value You're Adding

The After Renovation Value (ARV) HELOC is a home equity line based on what an appraiser says your home will be worth once the project is done.

A regular HELOC looks at your home today. This one looks at your home finished.

  • Second lien. It sits behind your first mortgage. No refinance.
  • A line of credit. It works like the HELOCs you've heard of.
  • Interest-only payments for the 10‑year draw period, then a 20‑year repayment period. Your payment stays low while the work is under way.
  • No standard draws. No draw-by-draw inspections and no construction consultant.
  • One final inspection to confirm the work is done.

Questions People Ask First

Will I lose my first mortgage?

No. This is a second loan that sits behind your first mortgage. There's no refinance, so your first mortgage, its rate and its payment stay exactly as they are.

How is this different from a regular HELOC?

A regular HELOC is based on what your home is worth today. This one is based on what your home will be worth after the project is finished. To get there, it adds two things: a review of the project, and an appraisal of your home "as finished."

Do I have to use Mars Construction?

No. Mars Construction can scope and build your project, and having the builder and the loan advisor on one team keeps things moving. If you already have a licensed contractor you trust, they go through the same project review: license and insurance, a short questionnaire, the cost estimate and the plans.

What kinds of projects qualify?

Kitchens, bathrooms, room additions, interior and exterior Remodels, roofing, siding, decks, porches, pools and outdoor improvements. ADUs qualify too. Full teardowns and investment-only properties don't.

What happens when I click Get Pre‑qualified?

It opens our secure online application with E Mortgage Capital. Fill it out and your Renovation loan advisor follows up to go over your home, your project and your options. Starting an application is not a loan approval.

What are the payments like during the project?

Interest-only during the 10‑year draw period, so the payment stays low while the work is under way. After that, the balance is repaid over 20 years. Interest-only payments don't pay down the balance, and the payment goes up when repayment starts. Your advisor shows you the real numbers for your line before you decide.

I'm buying a home. Can I renovate right after closing?

Yes. There's no waiting period after the purchase. Buy the home, then set up the line based on its finished value and start the project.

Can I use it on a rental property?

No. This program is for primary homes and second homes, on single-family or two‑unit properties. If yours is a rental, your advisor can walk you through other options.

What about the rate and costs?

Those depend on your credit, your home and the size of the line, so they're covered one on one. Your advisor lays them out before you decide anything.
